# Privacy Policy — MediaBot

Last updated: 23 November 2026

MediaBot is a personal, non-commercial tool used by its owner only.

## What it does
MediaBot records a history of media files the owner downloads,
and stores that history in the owner's own Google Drive account.

## Data accessed
MediaBot requests the `drive.file` scope. This grants access only to
files and folders that MediaBot itself creates. It cannot read, modify,
or view any other file in the user's Google Drive.

## Data sharing
No data is collected from third parties, sold, or shared with anyone.
All data stays within the owner's own Google account.
